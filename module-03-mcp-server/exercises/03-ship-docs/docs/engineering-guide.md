# Engineering Guide — DSS Pathfinder

## Power Systems

The station runs on a distributed power grid with four primary modules:
- **Habitat**: Life support, lighting, gravity generators (1000 MW capacity)
- **Lab**: Research equipment, containment fields (800 MW capacity)
- **Docking**: Bay doors, tractor beams, docking clamps (500 MW capacity)
- **Comms**: Long-range antenna, subspace relay (400 MW capacity)

Power can be reallocated between modules via the Engineering Console.

## Warp Drive

- **Type**: Alcubierre-class metric drive, Mark VII
- **Cruise speed**: 5 ly/hour
- **Maximum speed**: 8 ly/hour (sustained for max 4 hours)
- **Cooldown**: 30 minutes after maximum-speed operation
- **Fuel**: Deuterium-tritium fusion cells (current reserves: 340 hours at cruise)

## Shield Maintenance

Shields operate at variable efficiency based on power allocation and wear:
- **Above 90%**: Full protection against micrometeorites and low-energy radiation
- **70–90%**: Adequate for standard space travel
- **Below 70%**: Schedule maintenance immediately

Shield emitter inspection: monthly. Full recalibration: quarterly.
