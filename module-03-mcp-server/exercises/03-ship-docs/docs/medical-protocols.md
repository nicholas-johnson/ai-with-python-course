# Medical Protocols — DSS Pathfinder

## Medical Bay Location

Deck 2, Section C. Open 24 hours. Staffed during Alpha and Beta shifts. Gamma shift: emergency auto-doc available.

## First Aid

All crew members must complete basic first aid certification annually. Kits are located:
- Every corridor junction (wall-mounted, red cross marking)
- All labs and engineering spaces
- Bridge and shuttle bay

## Quarantine Procedures

Triggered automatically when biosensors detect:
- Unknown biological agents
- Radiation exposure above 50 mSv
- Contact with unclassified alien materials

Quarantine protocol:
1. Seal affected area (automatic bulkhead closure).
2. Activate decontamination cycle (UV + chemical wash, 15 minutes).
3. Medical team performs triage in hazmat gear.
4. Clearance requires two negative bioscan results, 12 hours apart.

| Equipment | Purpose | Status |

## Reporting

All medical incidents must be logged in the Medical Information System (MIS) within 1 hour. The Chief Medical Officer reviews all entries daily.
